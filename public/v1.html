Frequently Asked Questions.

Will it publish without my approval?+

Only if you let it. Approve mode holds every article, fix and outreach email for your sign-off. Autopilot acts and reports after the fact. Switch whenever you like.

Are the backlinks white-hat?+

Yes. Guest posts, resource pages and digital PR, earned through real outreach. No link farms, no paid schemes, and every placement is listed in your report.

Which CMSs does it work with?+

WordPress, Webflow, Shopify and Framer natively. Anything else through the API, or a review queue you copy from.

Can it really replace my marketer?+

For execution — research, writing, links, fixes, reporting — yes, and it does it every day. Strategy stays yours: you set the goals, the agent does the work.
